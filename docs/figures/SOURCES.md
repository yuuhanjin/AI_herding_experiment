# Figure sources

Most figures in this directory are generated from the versioned evidence in
`data/` by the offline scripts in `scripts/`.

`paper_fig6_above.png` and `paper_fig6_below.png` are cropped comparison panels
from Figure 6 of Alejandro Lopez-Lira, *Can Large Language Models Trade?
Testing Financial Theories with LLM Agents in Market Simulations* (2025),
[arXiv:2504.10789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10789). They are included only to
support non-commercial scholarly comparison in the progress-report insert.
They are not outputs of this repository.
